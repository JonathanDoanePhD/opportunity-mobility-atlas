Priority counties (≥ 2,000 children)

    How to read this. Need blends child poverty (2022), the CDC Social Vulnerability Index (2022) and low upward mobility. Supply counts youth-serving nonprofits (IRS categories O20–O23, O30–O31, O50) per 10,000 children. Priority = top quarter on need and bottom quarter on supply. Upward mobility is the average adult income percentile of children born 1978–83 to parents at the 25th income percentile. Economic connectedness is the share of high-income friends among low-income people (×2; 1 = class-integrated). "Beats the odds" marks high-poverty counties with mobility clearly above what their 1990 poverty, single-parent share and income predict, compared within their state.

    Limits. Nonprofit counts miss school, tribal, faith-based and government programs, so tribal and rural areas look emptier than they are. Connecticut isn't scored (its 2022 county boundaries changed). County figures say nothing about any one child. These are associations, not causal effects. Verify figures against the original sources before external use.

    Sources: Census SAIPE 2022 and boundaries; CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022; Opportunity Insights; Social Capital Atlas (Meta data license); IRS Exempt Organizations Business Master File.
